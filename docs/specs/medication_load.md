# Tool Specification: Medication Load & Polymedication Risk

**Owner:** Roua  
**Model Family:** XGBoost Multi-Class Classifier (Version B: after-stay features)  
**Bundle Location:** `models/roua/model_bundle.joblib`  
**User Role:** Doctors only (Clinical tool)

---

## 1. Clinical Question & Target Audience
- **Question Answered:** What is the expected medication load category for this patient at the end of their stay (Low, Medium, or High), and should the hospital clinical pharmacy prioritise them for a medication regimen review?
- **Primary Users:** Ward physicians, clinical pharmacists, and geriatric specialists.
- **Business Objective:** Polymedication management and pharmacy workflow prioritization. Patients taking 18 or more medications have elevated risks of adverse drug interactions, renal stress, and non-adherence. Hospital pharmacists review high-risk regimens first.

---

## 2. Target Classes & Thresholds
*(Extracted directly from `models/roua/model_bundle.joblib`)*
- **Three Medication Load Classes:**
  1. **Low (`low`):** 11 medications or fewer (`num_medications` $\le 11$).
  2. **Medium (`medium`):** 12 to 17 medications ($12 \le \text{num\_medications} \le 17$).
  3. **High (`high`):** 18 medications or more (`num_medications` $\ge 18$).
- **Version B (After-Stay):** Utilizes full hospital stay variables (including `time_in_hospital` and `num_lab_procedures`) to provide the most accurate post-stay pharmacy assessment.
- **Leakage Prevention Rule:** `num_medications` and individual diabetes drug columns are strictly excluded from inputs, as they define the target itself.

---

## 3. Model Performance & Metrics
*(Extracted directly from Roua's notebook, Cells 95–104)*
- **Model Version:** Version B (After Stay)
- **Accuracy on Test Set:** **60.9%** (95% CI: [60.0%, 61.6%])
- **Macro Precision:** 0.60
- **Precision for "High" Class:** **0.67** (2 out of 3 patients predicted "High" are true high medication load)
- **Severe Error Rate:** Only 7% cross-errors between Low and High (the model reliably separates extremes).

## 5. Result Display & Explainability
- **Predicted Level Badge:**
  - `Low Medication Load` ($\le$ 11 drugs, green badge).
  - `Medium Medication Load` (12–17 drugs, blue badge).
  - `High Medication Load` ($\ge$ 18 drugs, red/warning badge).
- **Class Probability Distribution:** Three-segment horizontal bar showing probabilities: $P(\text{Low})$, $P(\text{Medium})$, $P(\text{High})$.
- **Pharmacy Flag:** Highlighted if predicted class is `High` or $P(\text{High}) \ge 40\%$.

---

## 6. Recommendations & Clinical Banner
- **Decision Support Banner:**  
  *"Read this with care: Medication load estimates are generated by an XGBoost model (60.9% test accuracy) to help pharmacy teams triage regimen reviews. They are not a prescription or medication order."*
- **Recommendation Rules:**
  - **High Medication Load ($\ge 18$ drugs):** *"High medication load predicted. Consider a pharmacist review for potential drug-drug interactions, duplicate therapies, and renal clearance adjustments."*
  - **Medium Medication Load (12–17 drugs):** *"Moderate medication load predicted. Standard discharge medication reconciliation recommended."*
  - **Low Medication Load ($\le 11$ drugs):** *"Low medication load predicted. Routine medication counseling sufficient."*
