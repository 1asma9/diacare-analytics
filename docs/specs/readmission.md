# Tool Specification: Early Readmission Screening (< 30 Days)

**Owner:** Seif  
**Model Family:** Stacking Classifier (Meta-model: Logistic Regression combining Extra Trees, XGBoost, Tuned Gradient Boosting, Tuned Random Forest, Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, MLP Neural Network, etc.)  
**Bundle Location:** `models/seif/bundle.joblib`, `models/seif/metrics.json`  
**User Role:** Doctors only (Clinical tool)

---

## 1. Clinical Question & Target Audience
- **Question Answered:** Which diabetic patients being discharged are at elevated risk of unplanned early readmission (< 30 days), and whom should hospital staff prioritise for follow-up contact?
- **Primary Users:** Hospital physicians, discharge planning team, and post-discharge coordination nurses.
- **Business Objective:** Ranked follow-up priority list. Hospital teams have limited capacity to call discharged patients; the tool ranks patients so staff contact the highest-risk 20% first.

---

## 2. Model Performance & Metrics
*(Extracted directly from `models/seif/metrics.json`)*
- **AUC-ROC:** 0.6516 (~0.65)
- **AUC-PR:** 0.1804 (baseline prevalence is ~8.97%)
- **Recall at threshold:** 63.8% (0.6382)
- **Precision at threshold:** 13.0% (0.1296)
- **Flagged Population:** 44.2% of cohort flagged for follow-up at decision threshold
- **Base Rate (Cohort Prevalence):** 8.97% (0.0897)
- **Top 20% Capture / Rate:** Top 20% highest-risk patients have a 17.08% readmission rate (nearly 2x the base rate)
- **Bottom 40% Rate:** 6.01% readmission rate

---

## 3. Thresholds & Risk Bands
- **Decision Threshold ($\tau$):** `8.20%` (0.0820256)
  - Scores $\ge 8.2\%$: Flagged for priority post-discharge follow-up.
  - Scores $< 8.2\%$: Standard discharge instructions sufficient.
- **High-Risk Threshold:** `12.08%` (0.120835, top quintile)
- **Three Risk Bands:**
  1. **Low Risk (< 8.20%):** 55.8% of stays, 5.81% observed readmission rate. Standard discharge.
  2. **Elevated Risk (8.20% - 12.08%):** 24.2% of stays, 9.55% observed readmission rate. Scheduled follow-up call.
  3. **High Risk ($\ge$ 12.08%):** 20.0% of stays, 17.08% observed readmission rate. Early follow-up within 7 days, medication reconciliation.

## 5. Result Display & Explainability
- **Primary Display:**
  - Risk gauge showing predicted probability percentage (e.g. `7.0%` or `28.0%`).
  - Badge indicator: `Low Risk` (green), `Elevated Risk` (amber), or `High Risk` (red).
  - Priority Call Status: "Priority follow-up recommended" vs "Standard discharge instructions".
- **Top Contributing Factors ("What pushes the score up or down"):**
  - Displays top 6 features with the largest absolute effect relative to an average patient ($|\Delta| > 0.02$), based on logistic meta-learner weights.
  - Directional visual bars: Red (increases risk) / Green (lowers risk).

---

## 6. Recommendations & Clinical Banner
- **Decision Support Banner:**  
  *"Read this with care: This risk estimate is generated by a machine-learning model (AUC ~0.65) trained on historical records. It is a decision-support indicator to prioritise follow-up calls, not a clinical diagnosis."*
- **Recommendation Rules:**
  - **Score < 8.2%:** *"Standard discharge instructions are enough. No priority follow-up needed."*
  - **Score $\ge 8.2\%$ & < 12.08%:** *"Consider scheduling a follow-up call within 7–14 days to check glycemic control and medication adherence."*
  - **Score $\ge 12.08\%$:** *"Consider prioritising for early follow-up within 7 days, a post-discharge medication reconciliation review, and diabetes self-management education."*
