How it works

Dataset: 101 766 diabetic patient stays in 130 US hospitals (1999–2008) from the UCI "Diabetes 130-US hospitals" dataset. Split: 80% train + validation, 20% test (stratified, seed 42). Features: 194 columns after one-hot encoding — stay variables, 21 medications (binary), ICD-9 families, top-40 3-digit codes, admission type/source, medical specialty, payer code, constructed features (visits_prev, labs_per_day, etc.).

Final model: ensemble of HistGradientBoosting and RandomForest (300 trees, depth 18) with class weights tuned on validation to maximise macro F1.

Limits. The same patient can appear in both train and test (GroupShuffleSplit was not used). sejours_anterieurs uses encounter_id order as a proxy for chronology. "Autre" mixes very different outcomes. Data is US 1999–2008 and may not generalise to other settings or times. Use the score to guide early planning, not as a final discharge decision.
