How it works

The data: 69,970 first hospital stays of diabetic patients in 130 US hospitals (1999-2008). The target is a readmission within 30 days (9 % of the stays). The model combines a logistic regression, a gradient boosting model and Extra trees (stacking). The platform uses the 18 fields above, the notebook uses up to 152 variables and an ensemble of 8 models (AUC-ROC 0.66).

The follow-up threshold ({{(thr*100)|round(1)}} %) was chosen on the training data so that about 65 % of the early readmissions are caught.

Limits. The information available at discharge only predicts readmission moderately: every model we tried ends around 0.62-0.66 AUC. Precision is low because only 9 % of patients come back. Data is from 1999-2008 US hospitals and may not match another hospital today. Use the score to rank patients, not to make a decision on one patient.
