The Two Clinical Patient Profiles

K-Means clustering ($k=2$) combined with 18-dimensional Principal Component Analysis (PCA) revealing two distinct natural clinical archetypes among hospitalized diabetic patients.

Cluster 0: 62.0% (Routine) Cluster 1: 38.0% (Complex) PCA Dimension: 18D (90% Var)
Profile 0 62.0% of cohort

Routine / Moderate-Acuity Stay

Characterized by short, uncomplicated stays, manageable medication regimens, infrequent pharmacotherapeutic changes, and high home discharge rates.

Key Phenotype Markers
  • Length of Stay: Mean 3.1 days (vs. 4.3 days global avg)
  • Medications Administered: Mean 12.0 drugs (vs. 15.7 global avg)
  • Medication Modifications: Only 25% of stays had dose changes
  • Early Readmission (<30d): 7.7% (low readmission rate)
Clinical Protocol:

Standard discharge pathway with routine follow-up instructions and outpatient pharmacy scheduling.

Profile 1 38.0% of cohort

Complex / High-Acuity Polypharmacy

Characterized by extended stays, heavy pharmacotherapy, active drug adjustments, poor glycemic control, and high readmission probability.

Key Phenotype Markers
  • Length of Stay: Mean 6.3 days (double that of Profile 0)
  • Medications Administered: Mean 21.6 drugs (heavy polypharmacy)
  • Medication Modifications: 77% of stays required active titration
  • Discharge to Facilities: 27.3% (1.4x higher than cohort avg)
  • Early Readmission (<30d): 11.1% (elevated clinical risk)
Clinical Protocol:

Multidisciplinary discharge planning, pharmacist medication reconciliation, and post-discharge telephone contact within 72 hours.
