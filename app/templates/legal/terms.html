Terms of Service

University project — internal hospital staff clinical decision-support platform guidelines and terms of use.

1. Scope and Purpose

DiaCare Analytics is an internal hospital staff decision-support platform. It integrates six machine-learning models to assist physicians and admission staff in prioritizing patient care. The platform uses anonymized historical data from the UCI Diabetes 130-US Hospitals dataset and is for demonstration purposes only.

2. Not a Medical Device

DiaCare Analytics is not a certified medical device and must not be used for diagnosis, drug dosing, prescription orders, or definitive patient care decisions. All algorithmic outputs are exploratory statistical indicators derived from retrospective data.

All recommendations are phrased as "consider" and serve solely to assist staff with workflow prioritization. Staff must always apply independent clinical judgment.

3. Role-Based Access

Access privileges are governed by user role:

  • Doctors: Full access to all clinical tools, admission planning, and patient summary.
  • Reception / Admission Staff: Access restricted to Dashboard, Patient Summary, Length of Stay, and Discharge Destination. Clinical diagnostic tools are server-restricted.

4. Data and Privacy

No real identifiable patient data is collected, stored, or transmitted:

  • Form inputs exist only in volatile server memory for the duration of the request.
  • Batch CSV files are processed in-memory and never stored permanently.
  • No external network calls are made by this platform.
