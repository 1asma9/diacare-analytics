How to use


1. Choose the moment. At admission uses only what is known on arrival. Later in the stay also uses the number of lab tests and medications, which makes the estimate better.


2. Move the sliders. The result updates immediately.


3. Read the likely stay and the range. The “100 similar patients” bar shows how stays really turned out for patients with a similar profile.


4. Use Copy summary for the patient note, or Add to ward planner to see bed needs across the ward.



About the tool


StayCast learned from 69,970 past hospital stays of patients with diabetes. For each new patient it finds the past patients with the most similar profile and shows how long they stayed. It supports planning and does not replace clinical judgment.


When usedWhat it usesShort or long call is right


At admissionDiagnoses, procedures, medication changeabout 6 in 10 patients


Later in the stayAlso lab tests and medicationsabout 7 in 10 patients


Guessing without the tool–5 in 10 patients


Use it for planning. Individual stays vary a lot, and the estimate is most useful across a whole ward, where errors balance out. Short means 3 days or fewer, long means more than 3.


For developers
Target: time_in_hospital greater than 3 days. Pipeline: 80/20 split, 99th-percentile capping, log1p on skewed counts, correlation filter above 0.1. Model: AdaBoost, 200 stumps (test accuracy 61.0% at admission, 68.5% later; AUC 0.65 and 0.76), summed per variable into lookup tables, then probability-calibrated. The stay range comes from observed stays in each of ten risk groups. Rebuild with export_model.py.
